01 · AI COACH · LIVE

From wearable data
to personalized action.

Designing an AI-powered wellness coach that helps users understand what to do — not just what their data says.

Product DesignUX / UIAI ExperienceSmartwatch
THE OPPORTUNITY

Fitness Plans existed, but the experience needed to feel more personal.

The product vision evolved toward a more personalized wellness experience: users could either work toward a defined fitness goal or start with smaller everyday habits.

Goal-oriented
Fitness Plans · longer-term commitment · defined outcomes
Habit-builders
Habit Routines · smaller actions · daily consistency
FROM DATA TO ACTION
Wearable data→Wellness Balance→AI Coach→Personalized action

The goal wasn't to give users more data. It was to help them understand what they could do with it.

DESIGNING THE AI EXPERIENCE

AI wasn't just a chatbot. It needed a system of rules.

I worked on the experience and content constraints around AI-generated guidance: what the coach should say, how much it should say, when to ask for more performance, how personalized habits should behave, and how the system could scale as more experiences were added.

ContextUse relevant wellness and activity signals.
ConstraintsKeep guidance concise and actionable.
PersonalizationAdapt recommendations to user progress.
ScalabilityKeep styles, tones and rules configurable.
A KEY UX DECISION

Not every habit should become another task to track.

Some habits could be measured automatically by the wearable. Others depended on user behaviour and could not be reliably verified without manual input. Making every habit manually checkable would add friction — and could make reward systems easy to game.

Auto-monitored
Sleep · steps · activity

Eligible for streaks and rewards.
Behavioural
Reading · journaling · hydration · nutrition

Guidance without manual reward claims.

FEATURED UX CHALLENGE · WELLNESS PERSONA

A continuously changing metric doesn't need a continuously changing identity.

Because Wellness Balance updates through the day, changing the user's persona every time the score changed would weaken the meaning of a daily reward.

Solution: keep today's persona locked while the score is still changing, then reveal the previous day's final persona after the day ends. The persona could then become that day's watch face.

OUTCOME

AI Coach became a larger wellness ecosystem — not a single AI screen.

The experience is live and continues to evolve, with additional experiences and monetization opportunities planned to be introduced into the journey.

02 · WELLNESS BALANCE · LIVE

Making multiple wellness metrics understandable as one system.

Designing a holistic daily view that turns multiple wearable signals into a clear picture of overall wellness.

Product DesignInformation ArchitectureWearablesHealth
THE PROBLEM

Users had many metrics. They needed a way to understand how those metrics worked together.

The experience evolved from separate wellness signals toward a pillar-based system: Movement · Vitals · Restore.

INFORMATION HIERARCHY
Wellness Balance↓Pillar↓Contributor↓Metric↓Insight / Action
A DYNAMIC SCORE

The score changes through the day as data arrives.

The experience had to communicate a changing score without making the product feel unstable. The design also needed to account for different device capabilities and data availability.

SYSTEM DESIGN BEYOND THE UI
ConfigurablePillar weights, thresholds, ranges and scoring can be server-controlled.
Device-awareNewer devices can surface the experience on watch; older devices can remain app-first.
Data-awareUnsupported and unavailable metrics require different scoring behaviour.
ActionableRecommended actions can become information, reminders or nudges.
OUTCOME

Wellness Balance gives users a way to understand overall wellness through multiple contributing metrics rather than focusing on one metric in isolation.
