CASE STUDY · 02 · WELLNESS BALANCE · LIVE

Bringing many wellness metrics into one simple view.

Wellness Balance combines Movement, Vitals and Restore to give users a quick view of how they are doing.

ROLEProduct Designer
UX / UI · Interaction
PLATFORMMobile App + Smartwatch
CORE MODELMovement · Vitals · Restore
STATUSLive
01 · THE PROBLEM

Users had many metrics. They needed one simple way to understand them.

The Wellness Balance experience brings several wellness metrics together into one daily score. The final design uses Movement, Vitals and Restore so users can see the overall picture and then look at the metrics behind it.

02 · INFORMATION HIERARCHY

Start with the overall score, then show the details.

Wellness Balance↓Pillar↓Contributor↓Metric↓Insight / Action

This makes it possible to start with a simple overall view and then understand what is affecting the score.

03 · HOW THE DESIGN CHANGED

The design moved from one score visual to a clearer system.

We explored different ways to show the score and its parts. The final design uses Movement, Vitals and Restore with daily, weekly and monthly views.

EARLYThree-part score
MIDDLEPillars + contributors
FINALMovement · Vitals · Restore
04 · DAILY SCORE

The score updates through the day as new data comes in.

The score changes as the watch records and syncs data. The UI shows the contributors so users can understand what is affecting the score.

App

Daily, weekly and monthly views, contributors and more details.

Watch

Daily score and details on supported newer watches.

05 · HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

The score also needed to work across different devices and data.

Server controlledWeights, ranges, points and recommended actions can be changed without an app update.
Different devicesNewer watches can show the experience on the watch. Older watches can remain app-first.
Missing dataUnsupported and unavailable metrics are handled differently when calculating the score.
Recommended actionsThe system can show information, nudges or reminders when needed.
06 · MY ROLE

I designed the UI and helped shape the UX.

I worked with the team to simplify multiple wellness metrics into a clear pillar-based experience.

07 · OUTCOME

A simple way to see overall wellness.

Wellness Balance is live and gives users a way to understand their overall wellness through several metrics instead of looking at one metric at a time.
